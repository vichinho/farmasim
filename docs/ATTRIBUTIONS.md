distribuido bajo [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Fuente: [KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets — CesiumMan](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CesiumMan).
